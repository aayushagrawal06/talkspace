What is anxiety?

Understanding the condition and its prevalence.

Anxiety is a normal response to stress, but when feelings of worry, fear, or unease become persistent and interfere with daily life, they may indicate an anxiety disorder. According to the National Institute of Mental Health (NIMH), an estimated 19.1% of U.S. adults had an anxiety disorder in the past year, and 31.1% of adults experience an anxiety disorder at some point in their lives.

In New York City, the fast pace of life, high cost of living, and densely populated environment can contribute to elevated stress and anxiety levels. The NYC Department of Health and Mental Hygiene reports that approximately 18% of adult New Yorkers report anxiety symptoms.

Anxiety symptoms and precautions

Signs to look for, and when to get urgent help.

Common symptoms

  • Persistent worry or dread
  • Restlessness or feeling on edge
  • Difficulty concentrating
  • Sleep disturbances
  • Muscle tension or headaches
  • Rapid heartbeat or shortness of breath
  • Avoiding situations that trigger fear

Precautions

  • If anxiety stays intense and keeps you from daily life for many months, support can help.
  • If you have thoughts of suicide, call or text 988.
  • Anxiety interferes with work or school
  • Relationships are affected by worry
  • Physical symptoms persist without medical cause
  • You use substances to manage symptoms
